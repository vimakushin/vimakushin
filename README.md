Business and systems analyst with ten years of experience: five as an analyst in fintech and regulatory products, five in banks on FATCA/CRS automation. Now building tools for crypto and writing the spec before the code.

## Projects

- [**btc-what-if**](https://github.com/vimakushin/btc-what-if): what would your everyday habits be worth in Bitcoin, and how much would a different start date have changed that?
- [**before-you-sign**](https://github.com/vimakushin/before-you-sign): what does the signing request in your wallet actually say? Puts it into plain words; it translates and does not judge.
- [**wrong-network**](https://github.com/vimakushin/wrong-network): sent crypto on the wrong network, and can the coins be recovered? It does not promise recovery. *Work in progress: not yet tried on real users; the interface is in Russian only.*
- [**crypto-signal-agent**](https://github.com/vimakushin/crypto-signal-agent): what looks unusual in the market data today? Daily flags on revenue, market cap, volume and futures open interest, for a human to investigate; it does not predict prices. *MVP not yet confirmed: the unattended three-day run is unchecked.*
- [**crypto-portfolio-app**](https://github.com/vimakushin/crypto-portfolio-app): what is the average entry price across exchanges and wallets, when transfers are moves rather than trades? *Work in progress: entries are lost on reload; the interface is in Russian only.*
